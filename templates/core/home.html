Platform Capabilities & Engineering Highlights

Built to solve real data access friction for university research projects.

Strict Privacy Enforcement

Datasets are categorized as either Public or Private. Querysets and streaming downloads verify ownership at the Django view layer to prevent unauthorized data access.

Automated CSV Verification

Uploaded files are verified against size thresholds, encoding structures, delimiter sniffers, and tabular headers to reject corrupt files and safely parse metadata.

Instant Tabular Preview & Charts

Users can inspect the first 15–20 rows of any dataset before downloading, complete with column schema identification and sample metric trend visualizations.
